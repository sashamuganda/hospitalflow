## 2024-05-23 - Redundant List Filtering and Sorting in Build Method
**Learning:** Accessing computed properties (getters) that perform list filtering ($O(N)$) or sorting ($O(N \log N)$) multiple times within a `build` method, especially inside a `ListView.itemBuilder`, leads to $O(N \cdot M)$ or $O(N \cdot M \log N)$ complexity where $N$ is total items and $M$ is visible items. This causes unnecessary list allocations and expensive iterations on every frame.
**Action:** Cache the result of filtering/sorting getters in a local variable at the beginning of the `build` method to ensure the logic runs only once per frame.
